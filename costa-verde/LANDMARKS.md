# Costa Verde: 200 unique landmarks

One-off buildings and places, the equivalent of GTA V's and GTA VI's unique landmarks. Every name and design is
original. "Modelled on" gives the kind of real Florida or US place each one is based on; "Comparable GTA
landmark" shows the GTA V/VI equivalent where one exists (reference only, not copied). GTA VI references marked
"reported" come from trailer coverage on fan sites and aren't officially confirmed.

Data: `tools/data/landmarks.json` (generated by `tools/landmarks.py`, which checks there are exactly 200 unique).
