# Costa Verde roadmap

Everything is generated by code. Real public-domain US data (USGS imagery and elevation, Census TIGER/Line
roads) is used only as **training data**: the generators are tuned until their output has the same
statistics. No real place is copied.

## Phase A: World generator foundations (done)
- Island outline with three coast types: smooth Atlantic coast, barrier islands and bay, ragged mangrove Gulf coast.
- The Keys arc, lakes and wetlands. Every lake and wetland has its own water level.
- Terrain heights trained on 12 real elevation regions (`tools/terrain_stats.py`, `tools/train_terrain.py`).
- Street networks trained on 17 real road networks (`tools/road_stats.py`, `tools/train_streets.py`).
- Districts, buildings along street frontage, rural county roads, farmsteads, hamlets.
- 57 Easter eggs, rail lines, metro and stations.
- Preview tools: top-down map, Blender 3D renders, real-vs-generated comparison sheets.

## Phase B: Map realism pass (done, audited)
- Road network built as one system: A*-routed freeways through downtown and down the Keys, a continuous
  arterial grid with downtown boulevards and bay causeways, state roads between towns, local streets
  grown from the major roads, loose ends stitched, cut-off pieces connected. Audit: 100% of road
  length in one network, 0 km of road over water without a bridge, 97.6% of built-up land reached.
- Street styles trained in the in-game block setting; curvy suburbs; fields between county roads.
- Terrain trained on real elevation (fit error 0.29); smooth shores; flat wetlands.
- Metro follows the arterials with a station at every major crossing; rail routed over the terrain.
- Still to do: interchanges and on-ramps as geometry, tiny towns (Gator Hollow) get streets, real
  cul-de-sac share in suburbs (the stitch pass joins most loose streets on purpose).

## Phase B2: Map assembly (done, audited)
- All 200 unique landmarks (LANDMARKS.md) placed by siting rule: in town, open country, on the shore, offshore
  or on islets, with bridge landmarks on real bridges. Each has a real-size footprint and an access road;
  streets, lots and farms grow around them.
- Key Marisol: a town on the biggest island at the end of the Keys.
- Every building has a use in the real Florida mix (Census business counts by style). The 133 playable
  map markers (gun stores, mod shops, banks, police...) are spread through the towns.
- Audit adds: all 200 placed, none cut by roads, none with buildings inside, all reachable; uses match
  the real mix; every building has a use.
- Still to do: landmark-specific lot shapes (runways, car parks, stadium bowls), marker density in small
  towns, landmark labels decluttered at map scale.

## Phase B3: Roads finished, real building shapes (done, audited)
- Every freeway end joins another highway or becomes a town boulevard; one continuous north-coast road;
  rail lines joined; no loose major-road ends; freeways grade-separated (checked per road).
- Every building has a real multi-part shape: 9 house types drawn from a per-neighbourhood palette
  (ranch, L-ranch, colonial, courtyard, bungalow, split-level, duplex, townhouses, shotgun), tower,
  midrise, Art Deco, shopfronts, warehouse and campus forms, and use-specific forms (gas station,
  church, motel, self-storage, car wash, fast food, supercenter). Lots and farm buildings are checked
  corner by corner against every road.

## Phase C: Roblox world builder (needs you in Studio)
- Terrain builder ready: `src/shared/TerrainBuilder.luau` (4-stud voxels, per-biome materials, Florida palette,
  water levels, roadside ditches and wetland canals, levees, seawalls, wet sand, worn verges, dirt tracks).
- Vegetation and shore props ready: `src/shared/Vegetation.luau` places the models from
  `tools/blender/make_assets.py` (assets/roblox/*.fbx: 2 palms, live oak, slash pine, mangrove, palmetto,
  sawgrass, sea oats, dock, piling, buoy, riprap) by biome, clear of roads and buildings.
- To build in Studio: sync with Rojo; import assets/roblox/*.fbx with the 3D Importer into a folder
  ReplicatedStorage.Assets (keep the file names); then in the command bar, in edit mode:
  `require(game.ReplicatedStorage.Shared.TerrainBuilder).build()`,
  `require(game.ReplicatedStorage.Shared.Vegetation).build()` and
  `require(game.ReplicatedStorage.Shared.WorldBuilder).build()` and
  `require(game.ReplicatedStorage.Shared.LightingPreset).apply()`; save the place. The day/night cycle runs
  from ServerScriptService.Server.DayNight.
- Water variety: murky tint on swamp and lake water, foam on beach surf (Roblox has one global water style).
- Backyard canals in about a third of suburban neighbourhoods; big buildings slide up to a block to fit.
- Greybox ready: `src/shared/WorldBuilder.luau` builds roads (bridges, freeway viaducts with pillars, ramps),
  rail and the elevated metro, every building at its real footprint and height with pitched house roofs, and
  landmark site plans; buildings are atomic streaming models; streaming is switched on.
- Preview without Studio: `lune run tools/export-terrain.luau` then `python tools/blender/render_terrain.py`.
- Server script that runs the generator: terrain voxels in chunks (WriteVoxels), water at each lake's level,
  roads as parts or meshes, buildings as placeholders, streaming setup.
- First test: you press Play in Studio and send screenshots.

## Phase D: Blender asset kits
- Started: `tools/blender/detail_town.py` models every building in a test block (old town at 27740, 23180) as a
  custom detailed mesh from its real footprint and style (windows with frames, sills and shutters, shopfronts,
  awnings, balconies, Art Deco ledges and pilasters, hip/gable roofs with eaves, parapets, AC units, porches,
  garage doors, pools, driveways) plus sidewalks, curbs, lane markings and street lights. `--export` writes one
  Roblox-ready FBX per building (assets/roblox/buildings/, max ~15k triangles each). Next: continuous sidewalks
  with junction aprons, then the other styles' detail (towers, warehouses, campus) and the rest of the map.
- Building kits per style (glass towers, Art Deco hotels, stucco, houses, barns, warehouses), with windows and roofs.
- Palms and other plants, street furniture, vehicles, boats, Easter egg props.
- Several detail levels per asset; exported as FBX and batch-imported, then placed by name.
