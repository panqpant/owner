# Costa Verde roadmap

Everything is generated by code. Real public-domain US data (USGS imagery and elevation, Census TIGER/Line
roads) is used only as **training data**: the generators are tuned until their output has the same
statistics. No real place is copied.

## Phase A: World generator foundations (done)
- Island outline with three coast types: smooth Atlantic coast, barrier islands and bay, ragged mangrove Gulf coast.
- The Keys arc, lakes and wetlands. Every lake and wetland has its own water level.
- Terrain heights trained on 12 real elevation regions (`tools/terrain_stats.py`, `tools/train_terrain.py`).
- Street networks trained on 17 real road networks (`tools/road_stats.py`, `tools/train_streets.py`).
- Districts, buildings along street frontage, rural county roads, farmsteads, hamlets.
- 57 Easter eggs, rail lines, metro and stations.
- Preview tools: top-down map, Blender 3D renders, real-vs-generated comparison sheets.

## Phase B: Map realism pass (next)
- Curvy suburbs: collector loops with cul-de-sac "finger" streets, learned from the real suburb networks.
- Fields subdivide the land between the new county roads (they still use the old section grid).
- Terrain: fit coast and wetland bands, gentler beach berms and lake shores, river valleys.
- Barrier-island streets, highway interchanges and on-ramps, bridges with spans, rail stations with platforms.
- More hamlets, roadside stops (gas stations, motels, fruit stands), marinas, trailer parks.

## Phase C: Roblox world builder (needs you in Studio)
- Server script that runs the generator: terrain voxels in chunks (WriteVoxels), water at each lake's level,
  roads as parts or meshes, buildings as placeholders, streaming setup.
- First test: you press Play in Studio and send screenshots.

## Phase D: Blender asset kits
- Building kits per style (glass towers, Art Deco hotels, stucco, houses, barns, warehouses), with windows and roofs.
- Palms and other plants, street furniture, vehicles, boats, Easter egg props.
- Several detail levels per asset; exported as FBX and batch-imported, then placed by name.

## Phase E: Core gameplay
- Character, camera, vehicles, weapons, wanted level, saving, admin and debug tools. Logic is tested in Lune.

## Phase F: Living city
- Pedestrians and traffic driving on the generated road graph (it already exists as data), plus trains on the rail lines.

## Phase G: Story and missions
- Mission framework, dialogue and voice pipeline, the 3 protagonists, then the 500 hand-made missions and procedural mission templates.
