Pull the real Johto ground plan out of your HGSS cartridge

Every HGSS map is built from 32×32-tile pieces. Each piece has a grid saying which tiles are grass, water, ledges, doors or blocked, plus where its buildings stand. This page reads only that from your own ROM, so the Emerald version can match HGSS tile for tile.
